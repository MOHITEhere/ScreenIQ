About Saint-Gobain INDEC 
Saint-Gobain INDEC (International IT Delivery Centre) is the global technology hub for Saint-Gobain - one of the world's 
leading sustainable construction and building materials companies, operating for over 360 years. Based in Mumbai, INDEC 
powers IT solutions for Saint-Gobain businesses across 75+ countries, covering enterprise applications, digital 
transformation, data & analytics, cybersecurity and infrastructure.  
Joining INDEC means working on real, global-scale technology challenges with a structured career path, mentorship, and 
access to a global professional network.
